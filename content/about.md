## Hi, I'm Otmane 👋

By profession—and by main income—I am a **seasonal agricultural expert**. My life follows the rhythms of the crops and harvests, moving between agricultural regions, orchards, and packing stations. This is the work that pays the bills.

By passion, I'm a **Python developer**, **JavaScript artist**, **C hacker**, and **Scheme thinker**. Four languages, four moods: Python to get things done, JavaScript to make things move, C to remember what's really happening under the hood, and Scheme to keep my brain honest about what a program actually is.

I'm also an **Odoo expert** on the side, building and developing **Odoo modules**—somewhere between fieldwork and ERP modules, both somehow feel like debugging.

I'm an **old-school internet user** at heart—I got my **Baccalauréat in Mathematics**, then spent 2 years studying math in **CPGE** (classes préparatoires) before life took me toward the fields and the terminal instead.

I'm based in **Kenitra, Morocco**—and I love it, genuinely the best city in the world to me. That said, if you ask where I'm really from, my honest answer is **the internet**. That's my hometown.

### Code & Philosophy

- **Python:** My daily driver. Readable, pragmatic, gets out of my way so I can build.
- **JavaScript:** My canvas. I treat the browser like a sketchbook—animations, interactions, little bits of interactive art.
- **C:** The foundation of reality. **No language can ever replace C.**
- **Scheme:** Where I go to think clearly. Minimal syntax, maximal clarity—it keeps my mental models sharp.
- **Odoo:** My professional playground for business logic, modules, and making ERP systems behave.

I believe in a human, decentralized web: plain text, RSS feeds, no tracking, lightweight code, and zero corporate bloat.
