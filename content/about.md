## Who writes this

Tagine Times is a Moroccan satire site. We love this country enough to make fun of it — not the postcard version, the real one: the photocopies, the paperwork, the contradictions everyone complains about and nobody stops doing.

We write jokes, not news. Every joke starts from something real — a law, a statistic, a document, a quote — and we don't publish anything we can't back up. No fake scandals, no invented quotes, no manufactured outrage. If reality doesn't hand us a good enough punchline, we don't make one up.

Tagine Times isn't one person — it's a small group of Moroccan writers. No newsroom, no bylines, no single "editor." Just people who live here, deal with the same paperwork and the same contradictions, and decided to write about it instead of only complaining about it.

We're Moroccan too, so the joke includes us.
